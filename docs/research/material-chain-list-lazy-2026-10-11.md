## Problem, prior results and changed hypothesis

Actual NeoForge 21.1.248/MC 1.21.1 `BlockModel.getMaterial(String)` first strips an optional texture prefix, then eagerly creates a temporary `ArrayList<String>`, before `findTextureEntry` determines whether the local texture is direct. Alias chains check `List.contains`, append with `List.add`, and pass the original list to Guava `Joiner.join` for a cycle warning. All these paths and resolver callbacks are observable. Old #57 selective cache touched 2,203,625 material calls with 90.40% direct local and nevertheless worsened ElementsModel/bakeModels. Model material-result caches #152 (+22,825 ms hosted menu median), #170 (+2,097 ms) and #171 (+9,236 ms) were rejected. No result cache was reconsidered.

PR [#177](https://github.com/wachipayox/BootOptim/pull/177) remains an OPEN UNMERGED opt-in ThreadLocal list pool. Its HEAD/RETURN-only recycle leaks frames on exceptions and has unbounded retained deque/list capacity. Older hosted run [34160538431](https://github.com/wachipayox/BootOptim/actions/runs/34160538431) reported menu medians 88,556 ms control versus 86,932 ms candidate, but lacked current same-boundary net CPU/allocated-byte observations. It is not the mechanism in this investigation and the old wall contrast is not reused as evidence.

Diagnostic PR [#336](https://github.com/wachipayox/BootOptim/pull/336) after clock repair counted **1,396,811 original material calls / 1,333,196 direct (95.45%)** in its own hosted diagnostic population and one `Lists.newArrayList` factory per method call. These *counts* do not prove allocated heap or CPU savings (JIT escape analysis is possible). Neither #336 instrumentation nor parent-resolution candidate [#337](https://github.com/wachipayox/BootOptim/pull/337) was copied.

Changed trial premise: **only defer the temporary list's allocation until the first original consumer**, keeping fresh invocation-local state. A final Mixin extension recognized the exact patched `getMaterial` bytecode: one `Lists.newArrayList` factory storing local 2, one `List.contains`, one `Joiner.join(Iterable)`, one `List.add`. Replaced the initial factory with `null`; inserted `MaterialChainList.ensure` on each of those three receiver/argument loads. It returns the existing list or creates a fresh Guava `ArrayList`, never pools, memoizes or shares. Direct lookup bypasses the collection entirely; aliases, cycles, exceptions, reentrancy, source callbacks, current-generation texture values and logging remain in original order. Unknown target shapes, extra writes/uses/branch targets fail open to stock. No parent graph modifications, GL on workers, resource cache, loader ordering or publication changes.

Opt-in candidate `-Dboot_optim.lazyMaterialChainList=true`; control `false`. In both arms an identical `-Dboot_optim.profileMaterialChainStage=true` wrapped the **actual original `ModelBakery.bakeModels` invocation only once**, with current-thread CPU, allocated bytes and wall clocks (allocation-start query before CPU-start). No per-material expensive timing, replay of live callbacks or duplicated lookups. The resulting enclosing bake measure contains other bake work; it is NOT exclusive getMaterial CPU, cannot be added to listener timings, and is not a direct-menu saving claim.

## Binary decision, safety and reopening

**REJECT this lazy-list instruction rewrite for retention now.** Correctness-oriented actual-target tests and four hosted runtime cases pass; however, the required net CPU effect is of opposite signs in two comparable full-pack observations, while downstream wall in both comparisons favors control. The ~200 MB lower inclusive allocation readings are real observations but do not establish CPU or critical-path benefit and are too weak a reason to ship a targeted ASM rewrite under custom-Mixin/unknown bytecode compatibility risk. Do not revive #177 unbounded list pool, cache mutable materials, alter callbacks or piggyback #337. No persistent disabled candidate, new production optimization entry, change to integration runtime, physical-pack claim, or manual intervention required. Diagnostic commit is intentionally historical only.

Reopen only on a materially changed and explicitly lower-overhead mechanism or a **repaired same-workload material-only CPU attribution** with at least two controls and two candidates showing consistently positive *net optimized-operation CPU* inclusive of added guards/helpers, corresponding actual allocated bytes/GC and exact-pack semantics, or a credible verified hardware-side difference. A previous source-list factory count or older hosted menu contrast alone is not sufficient. The principal's only required decision is whether to merge this **documentation-only rejection PR** to preserve the record; no candidate promotion is requested.
