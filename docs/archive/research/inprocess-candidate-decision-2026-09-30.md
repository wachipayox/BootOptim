## New bounded protocol

`-Dboot_optim.benchmark.inProcessCandidates=true` enables a diagnostic volatile mask, initially zero. All four candidate properties and the production generated-item direct baker are enabled, but only the mask-selected candidate runs. Mask changes occur on the client thread, after the previous complete stock resource reload and two stable title seconds. There is no world entry, RRLS staging or custom partial reload. The instrumentation is not production code.

Initial generation is control. Then 21 menu reloads: one control warmup, Decocraft C/B/B/C, Sodium B/C/C/B, layer arithmetic C/B/B/C, Ferrite B/C/C/B with one same-mode conditioning reload before each of its four observations. The Ferrite primer accounts for capacity retained from the preceding generation. Expected total: 22 ResourceManager generations and 16 measured reloads. Each feature has two adjacent pairs in opposite order. The driver stops automatically; a failure invalidates the campaign.

Two CPU-clock reads per complete `ModelBakery.bakeModels`, never per model/quad, capture worker CPU and inclusive bake wall. Full reload wall starts at stock reload invocation and ends at its future completion. GC milliseconds and heap usage are separate. CPU, bake wall, reload wall and listener sums are not interchangeable. The mask's volatile read is symmetric diagnostic overhead; final retained candidates still require an uninstrumented physical gate.

`check_inprocess_trials.py` requires the exact request/completion ordering, masks, primers, CPU clock, success, terminal marker and 22 generations. Resource selection/order is independently checked against unchanged options. Duplicate, missing, failed, fallback or stale evidence is rejected. A completed launcher alone is insufficient.

## Predeclared disposition

CPU must improve in both opposite-order pairs and the median reduction must exceed max(200 ms, 1% of control bake CPU). A coherent full-reload regression exceeding max(1 s, 2% of control wall) rejects the candidate. A candidate meeting the CPU criterion advances to an uninstrumented final gate; this is not automatic startup promotion. Otherwise disposition is **no demonstrated practical win**, default off, documented rather than forgotten. Two pairs remain a bounded screening experiment, not a significance guarantee; disagreement or strong GC/system drift requires explicit inconclusive wording.

Laptop uses its verified existing Oracle 21.0.9, Xmx6G/user G1. This differs from the hosted fixture's Java 25.0.4 contract and is recorded rather than silently changing the user's JVM. Recovery restores the exact original packaged bootstrap and instance configuration; selected packs remain untouched. Laptop timeout is four hours for one JVM with 21 slow reloads, scheduler lifetime six hours. Hosted fixture must pass the same protocol before physical dispatch.
